About

Early in the circuit, on purpose

I'm a first-year Electrical Engineering student at Texas Tech University. I don't have a specific interest picked out yet, and I've stopped treating that as a problem to solve — it's just where a freshman year is supposed to be spent.

Right now that means working through the fundamentals — circuit analysis, physics, calculus — while poking at the things they lead to on my own time: a little Python, a little Arduino, reading about how power grids and embedded systems actually work.

What I can promise a team isn't a finished specialty. It's the habit underneath one: show up, ask the question that feels obvious, build the small thing, get slightly better than last week. This site will get more specific as I do.
